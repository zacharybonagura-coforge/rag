# Gru's Minion Field Handbook (Version 1.0)

## Mission Overview
This handbook is for minions assigned to Gru's house, lab, and lair. It covers daily duty, equipment, bananas, and what to do when a plan fails. Read it before your first shift. Keep one copy in your pocket.

Gru's work changes from week to week. Some weeks the team builds a freeze ray. Some weeks the team babysits. The rules in this book stay the same. A minion who follows it is useful. A minion who ignores it will sort socks.

The house is a home and a workplace. Children may be present. Do not test weapons in the living room. Do not store bananas in the vault. Do not teach the girls how to fire the freeze ray.

## Daily Operations

### Morning Briefing
Report at 7:30am. Wear goggles. Bring your own banana. Gru speaks first. Dr. Nefario speaks second if something is on fire. If Gru is still in his robe, wait.

The briefing names the target, the vehicle, and today's duty. Banana duty means you do not touch the freeze ray. Lab duty means you do not open the vault. Child watch means you do not mention world domination in front of the girls.

Late minions stand in the back. Three late arrivals in one week means you clean the antifreeze tank with a toothbrush. The toothbrush is not returned.

### Laboratory Duty
The lab is Dr. Nefario's floor. Do not press a button unless he points at it. Do not taste a beaker. Do not call a glowing liquid juice. If a machine hums, step back. If it screams, get Nefario. If Nefario is already screaming, get Gru.

Keep the freeze ray on the marked rack, safety cap on, barrel at the brick wall. The shrink ray stays in the locked cabinet. Only Gru and Nefario have the key. Label every crate. Unlabeled crates are hostile. Do not store lunch next to plasma. Wipe the bench at the end of the shift.

### Banana Service
Bananas are a workplace supply. The main crate is in the kitchen. The backup crate is in the garage behind the rocket fuel. Do not hide a stash in the air ducts.

Each minion may take two bananas before noon and one after the mission. A failed mission still gets the afternoon banana. A success may get a bonus banana, but only after Gru says so. Brown bananas go to the smoothie pitcher. Green bananas stay in the crate.

## Equipment
Every field minion carries goggles, gloves, and a communicator. The communicator is for mission talk, not singing and not impersonating Gru.

Only assigned drivers start an engine. Passengers buckle in. No standing on the roof during takeoff. If you crash a vehicle, file a report and walk home.

Costumes stay on the numbered hooks. The cook outfit is for the kitchen. The tux is for formal theft. Return costumes clean. Freeze-ray frost on a sleeve is not clean.

## Rules of Conduct
When Gru asks for a status, give the status. "Banana" is not an answer. When a child asks what you are building, say it is a surprise.

Do not fight in the lab or near the girls. Pillow fights are allowed in the bunk room after 8pm if no inventions are involved. Water balloons are never allowed near the electrical panel.

If Vector, El Macho, or an unknown visitor appears at the door, do not let them in. Get Gru. Do not accept a gift crate. Minions work for Gru. If a new master offers a bigger banana, report it.

## Emergency Procedures
If the freeze ray fires indoors, clear the room and mark the ice. Do not lick it. If the shrink ray fires indoors, do not step on anything that squeaks. Put small objects in the red bin and wait for Nefario.

If the house alarm sounds, close the vault, hide the plans, and take the girls to the orange couch. If the rocket starts without a countdown, pull the red handle in the garage and leave.

Fire, flood, and escaped animals are get-Gru events. After any emergency, write the log before you eat. When in doubt, find Gru, keep the children safe, and leave the bananas where you can count them.
