# Harbor Bike Shop Handbook (Version 1.0)

## Hours
The shop is open Tuesday through Saturday from 10am to 6pm. We are closed Sunday and Monday.

## Rentals
City bikes rent for $25 a day. Electric bikes rent for $45 a day. A photo ID is required.

## Repairs
A flat tire fix takes about 20 minutes. Tune-ups are usually done the same day if you drop the bike off before noon.

## Helmets
Every rental includes a helmet. Staff can help adjust the straps before you leave.

## Returns
Bring rentals back 15 minutes before close. Late returns are charged for an extra day.

## Lost and Found
Ask the front desk for lost items. We keep found keys and bottles for two weeks.
